About Keelapps

Keelapps makes apps for the people who run Jira, Confluence and Bitbucket Cloud. We come from years of running Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket and the build pipelines around them. The apps follow habits from that work: every change is fully tested, each app asks for the narrowest access that does its job, and each product page says what the app does not do.

Where your data goes

All fourteen apps are built on Atlassian Forge and declare no outside network access. There is no Keelapps server behind them, so your data stays in your Atlassian site and never reaches us. The twelve Jira and Confluence apps carry Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian badge on the Marketplace.

AccessLens and Releases only read: they change nothing in your site, so you can run them on production. Curator, which does change pages and labels, shows a preview of every change before it runs and can undo a whole batch.

How we check our work

Every app's repository runs a test check on each change, and the check fails if the tests cover less than 100% of the code, counting lines, branches, functions and statements. Each live app has its own documentation, its own privacy policy and a one-minute demo recorded on a production install.

We add a how-to guide to our guides every week, and we write about Jira and Confluence administration on Atlassian Community.

Support

Questions, bugs and feature requests go to our support portal, which runs on Atlassian's Jira Service Management. Whoever answers your request also writes the code, so a bug report goes straight to someone who can fix it. Security reports have their own process, with an acknowledgement within 3 working days: see the security policy.

The company

Keelapps is an independent Atlassian Marketplace Partner, not affiliated with Atlassian. Every Jira and Confluence app is free for sites up to 10 users, and the two Bitbucket apps are free on every site. Larger sites pay per user through Atlassian, on the same invoice as Jira and Confluence; pricing has the details.
