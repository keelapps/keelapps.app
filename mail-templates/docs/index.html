Get started with Mail Templates

Mail Templates is an extra notification channel. Jira keeps sending whatever its own notification scheme sends; the app adds a templated message on top, aimed at people who would otherwise get nothing. It cannot stop Jira's own mail, because Forge gives apps no way to. Plan around that from the start: see duplicate emails.

Turn it on for a project

You need to be a project administrator for that project, or a Jira administrator. The check runs as you, not as the app.

  1. Open the project, then Mail Templates in the project's tab bar (it may be under More).
  2. Pick one of the three event tabs and tick Send an email when…
  3. Write a subject and body, or press Start from an example to load a working template.
  4. Choose the recipients, then Save.

A project with no configuration sends nothing. Remove from this project deletes that project's rules entirely.

Every page

  1. Which Jira events send an email

    Mail Templates for Jira sends on three events: issue created, status changed and comment added. Each has its own template, recipients and switch.

  2. Template variables and formatting

    Every variable a Mail Templates subject or body can use, such as {{issue.key}} and {{transition.to}}, and the small markdown subset the body accepts.

  3. Choose who gets the email

    Send a Mail Templates email to the reporter, assignee, watchers, voters, named people or Jira groups. Each person gets one copy, and nobody who cannot see the issue gets one.

  4. Site switch and hourly email limits

    The Mail Templates admin page: a site-wide off switch, hourly email limits per project and for the whole site (200 and 1,000 by default), and the projects that have rules.

  5. Read the send log

    The Mail Templates send log lists a project's last 50 events and what happened to each: sent, turned off, no matching rule, hourly limit reached, blocked by Jira and more.

  6. Why didn't the email arrive?

    A checklist for when a Mail Templates email did not arrive: the rule and site switches, whether the event matched, recipient access, hourly limits and the subscription.

  7. Stop people getting two emails for one change

    Why a watcher can get both Jira's notification and a Mail Templates email for the same change, and how to turn the matching event off in the notification scheme safely.

  8. Permissions, and what Mail Templates will never do

    The four scopes Mail Templates for Jira requests, none of which can change Jira data, and the features it will never have: attachments, a custom sender, unsubscribe management.

  9. Mail Templates questions

    Short answers about Mail Templates for Jira: editing Jira's own emails, duplicates, automation usage, people outside Jira, attachments, restricted comments and subscriptions.
