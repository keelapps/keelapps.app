Schedule scans and keep snapshots

Schedule scans

In Settings, set the cadence to weekly or monthly and pick the day. Monthly runs can be set from the 1st to the 28th, so every month has that day. Scheduled scans run in UTC. A scheduled run that collides with a manual one is skipped rather than queued, so two scans never race.

How many snapshots are kept

Snapshots are kept until you delete them, up to a limit you set between 1 and 12 (the default is 12).

At the limit, AccessLens refuses a new snapshot rather than deleting an old one. Audit evidence is never discarded automatically: export and delete a snapshot yourself to make room.

A snapshot that an open review uses cannot be deleted until the review is signed off or deleted. A signed-off review keeps its own copy of what it showed.
