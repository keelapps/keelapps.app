AccessLens questions

Does AccessLens change any Jira permissions?

No. It reads permission schemes, project roles and group members, and makes no write requests to Jira. What it stores is its own snapshots, reviews and settings, in Atlassian-hosted app storage.

Who can open AccessLens?

Site administrators. AccessLens lives on a Jira admin page (Settings → Apps → AccessLens), and Jira shows admin pages to site administrators only. A site administrator can also turn on a Groups and members page in project settings (AccessLens → Settings → Project settings page). It shows a project's admins the groups their project uses and who is in them. It starts off.

Can AccessLens show who had access on a past date?

Yes, if a snapshot was taken then. Jira's live data only shows today, and its audit log lists events rather than the state on a given day. Schedule weekly or monthly scans so the dates you will be asked about are covered.

Does a snapshot include archived projects?

No. A scan reads every live project. Archived projects are not scanned.

Does AccessLens know who has a Jira licence?

No. A snapshot does not record who holds Jira access, so a permission granted to every licensed user is listed apart, with a note that it applies if the person has Jira access.

Does any permission data leave Atlassian?

No. The app has no external permission, so Forge blocks any outbound call, and CSV exports are built in your browser.

Is AccessLens free?

It is free on sites with up to 10 users. Above that it is priced per user; see pricing.
