Find projects open to anonymous users

Some permission grants do not name people at all. They name an open-ended group: anyone on the internet, everyone with Jira access, or every portal customer. AccessLens flags each of those grants in a snapshot so they do not hide among the named ones.

The flags

FlagWhen it appearsSeverity
Anonymous users can browse this projectthe permission scheme grants Browse projects to Anyone, so people who are not logged in can read the project's issueshigh
Anonymous users hold permissionAnyone holds any other permissionmedium
Granted to every licensed usera permission is granted to an application role, which means everyone with access to Jiramedium
Service desk portal customers are granted this permissiona permission is granted to portal customers of a service projectlow

Where you see them

  • Explore → By project: a banner above the result counts the project's risk findings and lists the first five.
  • Explore → By group: each project row carries its risk badges.
  • Reviews: projects with risk findings sort first, and the list can be filtered to show only those.
  • CSV exports: the snapshot export and the by-group and by-project exports have a Risk flags column.

Before turning on Rovo, the anonymous and every-licensed-user flags are the ones to clear first. The guide Before you turn on Rovo walks through it.
