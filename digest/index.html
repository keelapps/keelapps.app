For people who want the week's changes, not every event

Six emails about one issue, folded into one summary.

Jira notifies per event: one issue is commented on three times, changes status twice and mentions you once, and you have six emails. Digest folds everything that changed in a period into one structured summary — grouped how you choose, delivered to an in-app inbox, read when you choose. It never filters, rewrites or intercepts Jira's own notifications.

The gap this fills

Jira's personal notification settings can silence email, but only event by event — there is no native way to ask for “everything that changed against this JQL, once a week, in my time zone”. The usual workaround is a reporting suite: dashboards, gadgets, sometimes an external server, when all that was wanted was a summary that arrives on its own.

Capabilities

What it does

One subscription, one summary

Pick a JQL query, a cadence — daily at your own hour, or weekly on a chosen day — a time zone and a grouping. From then on, each period folds into a single digest.

Everything that changed, classified

Issues created, status transitions, assignments to you, priority changes, and comments that @mention you — each listed once, grouped by project, assignee or status.

See only what you may see

Digests are re-filtered against your own permissions the moment you open them. Anything you cannot see is stripped and counted — the check fails closed, never leaks.

Honest about silence

A period with no changes delivers nothing, unless you ask for empty digests. JQL that Jira rejects pauses the subscription and shows Jira's own error message rather than failing silently; a valid query that simply matches nothing is reported as an empty period.

Truncates honestly

When more than 50 issues changed, the digest shows the 50 most notable, says how many matched in total, and gives a deep link that opens the full result in the issue navigator.

Runs entirely on Atlassian infrastructure

Built on Forge with read-only Jira scopes and no external calls. The app cannot change an issue and cannot send your data anywhere.

Walkthrough

The Monday-morning status summary, end to end

  1. Subscribe once

    One JQL query for the team's project, weekly on Monday at 08:00 in your own time zone, grouped by status.

  2. The week rolls itself up

    Each run covers the window since the last successful one — created, moved, assigned, mentioned — so a late run covers the gap instead of dropping it.

  3. Open the inbox before standup

    What reached Done, what is stuck, who was assigned what, where you were mentioned — one page instead of a week of emails, read before anyone asks.

  4. Each digest is yours alone

    A digest is personal. It is generated with the app's own permissions, so when you open it, it is re-filtered against yours at that moment: entries you may not see are stripped and counted, never leaked.
