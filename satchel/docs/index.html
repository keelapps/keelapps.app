Get started with Satchel

Satchel does two things: it gets attachments out of Jira in bulk, and it tells you what is in there.

On an issue, Satchel is in the right column under Apps: open it there. The same panel is also under the View app actions button below the issue title (the round icon, not the “+”).

This is the most common “is the app broken?” moment after installing, and it is where Jira puts apps, not something Satchel chooses.

Where it lives

  • The issue panel: Apps → Satchel in the right column of any issue. Lists the issue's attachments and downloads them, one at a time or as one zip.
  • The project page: Project → Satchel. Browse, JQL, the inventory, duplicates and storage reports for that project.
  • The admin page: Apps → Satchel, for site administrators: settings, the deletion switch and the audit trail.

A panel stuck on “Loading Satchel…” is a bug worth reporting, not slowness: every call the interface makes has a time limit and turns into a visible error.

What is free

Sites with 10 users or fewer pay nothing. Above that, two things stay free on every install: the single-issue zip and the direct single-file download. A licence adds cross-issue and project-wide archives, the project inventory, the duplicate and storage reports, CSV export, and deletion.
