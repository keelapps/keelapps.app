Review thank-you terms

Last updated: 20 November 2026

If we have invited you by email, we will send you a US$15 gift card as a thank-you for an honest review of one of our apps on the Atlassian Marketplace.

  • Any rating counts. A critical or one-star review gets the same thank-you as a five-star one. We never ask you to change a review.
  • It has to be your own honest opinion, written after using the app on your site, and it has to follow the Atlassian Marketplace Terms of Use.
  • Please mention in your review that you received a gift card from us for writing it.
  • One gift card per person. The offer is for people we have invited by email and covers reviews posted by 31 December 2026.
  • Once your review is visible on the Marketplace, reply to our email. We send the gift card by email within 5 working days. Where you can choose, you may take it as a charity donation instead.
  • Keelapps staff, and people acting for Keelapps, cannot take part. Your employer's rules on accepting gifts apply to you.

Questions: hello@keelapps.app.
