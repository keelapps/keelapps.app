See who can reach what. Change nothing.

AccessLens is a read-only permission audit for Jira and Confluence Cloud. It reports which groups and people can reach each project, space and restricted page, keeps a snapshot for your auditor, and never edits a permission.

Below the waterline

Every app is built the same way, whichever gap it fills.

Admin tools touch permissions, pages and people's inboxes. These four things hold for every app we publish.

  1. Runs inside your tenant

    Built on Atlassian Forge, declaring no external network access. Your data is never sent to us, or to anyone else.

  2. Installed and billed through Atlassian

    Every live app is free for sites up to 10 users. Above that it is priced per user and billed by Atlassian, not by us. See every price.

  3. Honest about its limits

    Each product page compares the app with native Jira or Confluence and the alternatives — including what it does not do.

  4. A demo before you install

    A one-minute demo for every live app, recorded on a production install rather than mocked up.

Availability

Free for sites up to 10 users.

Every live app has a 30-day free trial. Above ten users it is priced per user and billed by Atlassian, on the same invoice as Jira and Confluence.
