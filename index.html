macOS

Keelhaven — backup for your Mac. The folders you cannot lose, encrypted before they leave the machine, on storage you own. Free and open source, no account, no telemetry, and an open backup format you can restore from without us. Developed in the open, so its source, bugs, and feature requests live at github.com/keelapps/keelhaven, and its privacy policy and support details at keelhaven.app.
